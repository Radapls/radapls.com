# My experience at the Alcaldía de Cali

I joined the Alcaldía de Santiago de Cali as a Delivery Unit member in March 2019. The Delivery Unit was the strategic monitoring office of the city government, tracking the progress of the administration's development plan and flagship projects.

### Among my main responsibilities were:

- Monitoring and following up on strategic indicators of the municipal development plan
- Preparing progress reports for the mayor's office and secretariats
- Supporting coordination between government secretariats and executing units
- Verifying delivery of flagship city projects

# My experience at the Instituto de Estudios Interculturales

I joined the Instituto de Estudios Interculturales (IEI) as a Social Researcher in June 2020. The IEI is a research institute based in Cali, Colombia, focused on intercultural studies, education and community development across the Pacific region.

### Among my main responsibilities were:

- Designing and applying qualitative research instruments (interviews, focus groups, field diaries)
- Systematizing and analyzing field data for research reports
- Co-authoring technical documents for academic and institutional audiences
- Coordinating fieldwork with community partners

# My experience at the Misión de Observación Electoral

I joined the Misión de Observación Electoral (MOE) as a Research Assistant in January 2018. The MOE is a Colombian civil-society organization that observes electoral processes and promotes transparency and citizen oversight of democracy.

### Among my main responsibilities were:

- Supporting electoral observation research and data collection
- Systematizing information for reports on electoral processes
- Assisting in the preparation of technical and public documents

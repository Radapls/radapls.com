# My experience at NT Consult

I joined NT Consult as a Frontend Developer in February 2023. NT Consult is a Brazilian IT consulting firm with clients across Latin America, and I worked there as a contractor embedded in different client teams — most notably BirdiRx, where I spent almost two years (you can read about that experience [here](../birdi/)).

Through NT Consult I also had the chance to work for other clients, including **Globo**, contributing to frontend development with Angular and collaborating with distributed teams across Brazil and Colombia.

### Among my main responsibilities were:

- Developing and maintaining frontend features for client projects
- Collaborating with distributed, multicultural teams
- Translating client requirements into working UI features

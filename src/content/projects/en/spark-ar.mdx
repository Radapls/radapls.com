---
title: 'Spark AR Filters'
---

These are some filters I built with Meta's Spark AR framework. The idea was to create augmented-reality visuals that could run inside Meta's apps (Instagram, AR glasses). The project was discontinued at the end of 2024 — see the [announcement](https://spark.meta.com/blog/meta-spark-announcement/). Since the filters are no longer available, here are some captures of them running inside Instagram. Some were published as teaser campaigns for Wibx, like _The Next Step_.
